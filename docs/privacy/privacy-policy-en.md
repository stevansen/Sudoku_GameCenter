# Privacy Policy — Sudoku

Effective: 24 August 2026
Controller: Stefan Hellweger, stefan.hellweger@mac.com

This document must be reachable at a public URL; App Store Connect requires it.

## In short

The app collects **no personal data** and sends nothing to us. There are no ads,
no tracking, no analytics SDKs and no user accounts.

## What data exists and where it stays

**On your device**: saved games, notes, statistics, points and settings. This data
leaves your device only if iCloud is enabled.

**In your iCloud** (only with iCloud enabled): the same data is stored in your
**private** iCloud area through Apple's key-value store
(NSUbiquitousKeyValueStore) so you can continue on
another device. We have no access to that database, neither technically nor
legally. Apple operates the storage and Apple's privacy policy applies. You can
turn synchronisation off at any time in your device's system settings.

**In Game Center** (only if you sign in): the app submits your scores, best times
and achievement progress to Apple's Game Center service, where they appear under
your Game Center profile. That service is Apple's; we receive no copy and have no
access to your profile. Game Center is optional — the app is fully usable without
signing in.

## No sharing with third parties

No sharing takes place because no data reaches us. The app contains no ad
networks, no analytics services and no third-party libraries.

## Deletion

Deleting the app removes all local data. Data held in iCloud can be removed under
*Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage*. Game Center data is managed in
your device's Game Center settings or at privacy.apple.com.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data. It has no chat, no web
access and no purchases.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published here and noted in the App Store
listing.
